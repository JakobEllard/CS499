I figured I probably better include a little cheat sheet so you can have something to refer to regarding all the terminology I'm going to be using

Training Block- a typically 8 week progression followed by a deload week where you lower the weight to recover accumulated fatigue
Day- an actual day of training, or just one workout
Set- a group of single repetitions, for instance, doing 10 bicep curls
Rep- a single repetition, one bicep curl
RPE- rate of percieved exertion, on a scale from 1-10 of how difficult the set was, used to determine progression
Surplus- eating more than maintanence calories to gain fat and muscle, but at a much quicker rate, allowing for faster progression
Deficit- eating less than maintenance to lose fat, but also increases mental fatigue and causes progression to slow, sometimes even grinding to a halt

Day types (Upper, Lower, Push, Pull, Legs, Full body) - just a general idea of what excercises I'll be doing, not super strict so i dont have a predetermined set of excercises
