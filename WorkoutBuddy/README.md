Workout Buddy 

Workout Buddy is a digital logbook that tracks a user's sets, reps, and adjusts their next workout automatically based on several characteristics. There are existing solutions but the one I plan to model my system off of requires a subscription. I am making this specifically for myself so the adjustments will be based on my personal needs. In particular, I want to automate the progress of setting a rep/weight goal for my next workout based on the previous day's rate of perceived exertion or RPE (a measurement of how hard the set was from 1-10), how deep  I am into a training block, and whether I am in a calorie deficit or surplus. It will eliminate the guesswork of trying to determine if I am progressing properly and help mentally by setting goals. While this may be useful to others, it is something I have wanted to make for a long time and being able to tailor it to myself means I can avoid solutions that are costly and unintuitive for me.

Core Features:
1. Create a training block with details (deficit or surplus
2. Add a day to a training block
3. Edit day - 
      type - upper, lower, etc
      excercises - curls, bench
      sets - weight x reps
      difficulty - 1-10
4. Carry over exercises from previous days and make recomendations

I believe this should be feasible to complete in 8 weeks, it will be a lot of components, information drawn from a database, and then displayed as a recommendation based on a simple formula. The logbook portion is absolutely necessary, recommendations could be cut if implementation is too complex or time consuming, I could go further and display a graph for weight progression, add a deload period at the end of a training block, or automate when to end a training block if time allows. 

The Information I see myself needing to store are the date in the training block, the exercise names, sets, and then I will probably set up a Boolean for whether the user is in a deficit, and how difficult the current block is.
